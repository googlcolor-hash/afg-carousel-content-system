# QA Checklist

- [ ] Cover hook is readable in under 2 seconds.
- [ ] One idea per slide or beat.
- [ ] Text fits on mobile.
- [ ] Safe zones are clear.
- [ ] Captions do not collide with UI or CTA.
- [ ] Claims are supported or phrased safely.
- [ ] Industry-specific compliance risks are checked.
- [ ] Asset rights and AI disclosure notes are filled.
- [ ] Platform dimensions and filenames are correct.
- [ ] One primary CTA is present.
